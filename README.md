### <samp>T H E &nbsp; P R O F I L E</samp>
*a person, before the portfolio*

</div>

<br/>

> **She builds because she keeps noticing the gap between what a system does and what a person actually needs — and can't leave it alone.**

<br/>

Jaskirat Kaur is studying Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, which sounds tidy on paper and rarely is in practice. Most of what she's learned came from a model that didn't do what she meant, an API that broke at the worst possible moment, a deployment that needed one more fix than expected.

She's drawn to the unglamorous middle of building — the part after the idea and before the demo, where the real decisions get made. That's also where she's happiest.

Off the page, she's part of **GDG on Campus**, a circle of people doing the same kind of figuring-out, in public, together.
